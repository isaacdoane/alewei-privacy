Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email isaacdoane7@gmail.com. Do not send API keys, transcripts or confidential video content.

Captions cannot be read

Try opening YouTube’s transcript panel. If captions are absent or a declared track has no usable segments, Alewei can offer the separately confirmed Gemini video fallback. A normal network error does not automatically start that fallback.

How much is free?

The extension is free. Provided AI access permits up to seven new successful summaries per installation and UTC day for videos up to 100 minutes, while credit is available. Cached results do not consume another summary. Abuse controls can impose additional limits, including on shared networks.

Can I use my own key?

Yes. Save an Anthropic API key in Settings. It is stored in your Chrome profile and sent directly to Anthropic, never to Alewei or Google. Anthropic bills your usage under its terms. Gemini fallback still depends on Alewei’s available transcription credit.

Language

Choose Automatic, Deutsch or English in Settings. Automatic selects German for a German browser language, otherwise English. Changing language does not automatically start a paid request.

How do I delete my data?

Reset consent in Settings. The extension requests server-side deletion before clearing its installation identifier. Failed deletion is shown. Short-lived anti-abuse counters and anonymous cost aggregates remain as explained in the privacy policy.

Incorrect summaries

Captions and AI output can be inaccurate. Use timestamps to check the original video.
