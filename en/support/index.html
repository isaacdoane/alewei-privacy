Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email isaacdoane7@gmail.com. Do not send API keys, transcripts or confidential video content.

How do I start a video analysis?

Open a public YouTube video and click Alewei in the Chrome toolbar. After your consent, Alewei analyzes the video and creates an English or German summary in the side panel. Alewei processes public videos up to 100 minutes, excluding ongoing and upcoming live broadcasts. Check important claims in the original video. AI-generated transcripts and summaries can contain errors.

How much is free?

The extension is free. Provided AI access permits up to seven new successful summaries per installation and UTC day for videos up to 100 minutes, while credit is available. Cached results do not consume another summary. Abuse controls can impose additional limits, including on shared networks.

Can I use my own key?

When you use your own Anthropic API key, the extension sends the timestamped transcript created by Gemini and the information needed for the summary directly to Anthropic. Your key is stored only in your Chrome profile and is sent to Anthropic. Anthropic may bill you according to your plan. Video analysis with Gemini still uses the credit provided by Alewei.

Language

Choose Automatic, Deutsch or English in Settings. Automatic selects German for a German browser language, otherwise English. Changing language does not automatically start a paid request.

How do I delete my data?

In Alewei Settings, “Delete cached summaries” clears the result cache. “Delete consent and usage data” requests server deletion, then removes the local installation identifier and consent after confirmation. Alewei displays errors. To delete data stored by YouTube, use YouTube directly.

For requests outside the extension, include the support code from Settings. See the privacy policy for the retention periods of short-lived anti-abuse counters and anonymous cost aggregates.

Incorrect summaries

AI-generated transcripts and summaries can be inaccurate. Use timestamps to check the original video.
