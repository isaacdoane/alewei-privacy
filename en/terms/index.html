Alewei Terms of Use

These terms apply to the Alewei Chrome extension starting with version 1.0.

By using Alewei, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Alewei uses YouTube API Services for public video metadata. The Privacy explains what is processed and how you can withdraw consent.

Alewei creates AI summaries of public YouTube videos on request. Provided AI credit is limited. If you use your own Anthropic API key, your direct use is also subject to the terms and prices of your Anthropic account.

Check important statements against the original video. Alewei generates summaries and transcripts with AI. They may contain errors.
