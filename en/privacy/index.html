2. Requested processing

Only after your action, Alewei reads the current video ID/URL, title, channel, duration and available transcript with timestamps. Captions and AI output may be inaccurate. Public transcripts can contain personal data; Alewei does not separately classify it. Alewei does not use content for advertising, profiling or its own AI training.

3. Provided AI access after consent

After active confirmation of a specific disclosure, the extension sends video metadata and the available transcript over HTTPS through Alewei’s Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic for the requested summary. Alewei also processes a pseudonymous installation identifier. Its server stores the identifier’s SHA-256 hash, random request IDs, status and content-free success/quota records. Keyed network hashes provide abuse controls; raw IP addresses are not stored in the Durable Object.

Purposes are providing the requested summary, enforcing up to seven successful new summaries per installation and UTC day, handling concurrency and duplicates, preventing abuse and protecting finite provided credit. Summary processing relies on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; content-free cost/security controls rely on the legitimate interest described under Article 6(1)(f). Device access that is not strictly necessary for the expressly requested service relies on consent under section 25(1) TDDDG. Consent can be withdrawn for future processing.

4. Separately confirmed video fallback

When captions are absent or a declared caption track yields no usable segments, Alewei offers a separate versioned disclosure. Declining starts no video request. After confirmation, the extension sends validated video metadata to the Worker. The Worker constructs and checks the canonical public YouTube URL with YouTube, then sends it to Google Gemini for timestamped transcription. In provided-access mode the transcript is summarized by Anthropic. In BYOK mode it returns to the extension and is sent directly to Anthropic with your own key. Your Anthropic key is never sent to Alewei’s Worker or Google. This processing relies on separate consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. Your own Anthropic key

A valid saved key takes priority when normal captions are available. The extension sends the key, metadata, transcript and prompt directly to Anthropic over HTTPS. The Worker is not called for normal BYOK. The key remains in chrome.storage.local in the Chrome profile until you delete it or remove extension data. Anthropic’s account terms and charges apply. Alewei does not claim zero data retention at Anthropic.

6. Recipients and international transfers

Recipients are Cloudflare for network, Worker and Durable Object operation; Anthropic for summaries; and, after separate fallback consent, YouTube for public-URL verification and Google Gemini for transcription. These providers may process outside the EU/EEA. Transfers rely on the safeguards stated in the operator’s approved German policy, including applicable adequacy decisions or EU Standard Contractual Clauses under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR.

7. Storage and deletion

  • Worker transcripts, video metadata, prompts and responses: request memory only; no application content storage or content logs.
  • Installation/request/success records: deleted after successful Reset consent, otherwise after 35 days of inactivity.
  • Content-free installation/network daily counters: current and previous UTC day; not reset by deletion.
  • Anonymous daily success aggregates: at most 90 days.
  • In-flight reservation window: up to 300 seconds. Costs of dispatched work whose final billing is unknown remain conservatively included in anonymous cost totals; expiry is not a refund.
  • Anonymous cost totals and stop state: for operation of the finite provided credit.
  • Local key, settings and cache: until deleted in Alewei/Chrome or extension data is removed.

Reset consent requests server deletion before removing the local installation identifier. Network failure is shown. Deletion does not refund provider costs. For access or deletion outside the extension, email the controller and supply the support code shown in Settings. This is a SHA-256 hash, not your secret installation bearer or an API key. Do not send keys, transcripts or confidential content. Content-free admission logs contain only fixed route names, HTTP method and HTTP status; automatic invocation logs and subrequest traces are disabled. Provider platform metadata follows actual provider settings and contracts; request-level store:false is not a zero-retention guarantee.

8. Language and permissions

Automatic/Deutsch/English controls the interface and new summaries. Cached results are separated by output language. YouTube access reads the current video context/captions; api.anthropic.com serves BYOK requests; the fixed Alewei Worker serves provided access and fallback. storage keeps local settings/keys/cache, sidePanel displays results and alarms coordinates pending work. Local data may also be processed by Chrome or your operating system under your chosen settings.

9. Website and support
