2. Requested processing

Alewei detects the video ID from the active YouTube tab’s address. When you request an analysis in Chrome’s side panel and consent, the extension sends the ID and output language to Alewei’s Cloudflare Worker. The Chrome extension uses YouTube API Services for public video metadata.

Alewei can cache the completed summary with the video ID, title, channel, duration and output language locally in Chrome. You can clear the cache in Settings. AI-generated transcripts and summaries can contain errors and personal information. Alewei uses the contents to provide and display the requested analysis.

3. Quota and legal basis

Alewei processes a pseudonymous installation identifier. Its server stores the identifier’s SHA-256 hash, random request IDs, status and content-free success/quota records. Keyed network hashes provide abuse controls; raw IP addresses are not stored in the Durable Object.

Purposes are providing the requested summary, enforcing up to seven successful new summaries per installation and UTC day, handling concurrency and duplicates, preventing abuse and protecting finite provided credit. Summary processing relies on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; content-free cost/security controls rely on the legitimate interest described under Article 6(1)(f). Device access that is not strictly necessary for the expressly requested service relies on consent under section 25(1) TDDDG. Consent can be withdrawn for future processing.

4. Video analysis after consent

Before the first analysis, Alewei shows its privacy information and asks for your explicit consent. Settings let you withdraw consent for future analyses.

The Worker sends the video ID to the YouTube Data API and receives the title, channel, duration, visibility and live status. It processes verified public videos up to 100 minutes. The Worker sends the public video URL constructed from the ID to Google Gemini, which creates a timestamped transcript. With provided access, the Worker sends Anthropic the information needed to generate the summary. With a personal Anthropic key, the extension sends this information directly.

The Terms of use explain the use of Alewei. The YouTube Terms of Service apply. See the Google Privacy Policy for information about processing by Google.

5. Your own Anthropic key

When you use your own Anthropic API key, the extension sends the timestamped transcript created by Gemini and the information needed for the summary directly to Anthropic. Your key is stored only in your Chrome profile and is sent to Anthropic. Anthropic may bill you according to your plan. Video analysis with Gemini still uses the credit provided by Alewei.

Anthropic’s account terms, privacy policy and charges apply. You can remove the key in Settings or by clearing extension data.

6. Recipients and international transfers

Recipients are Cloudflare for network, Worker and Durable Object operation; Anthropic for summaries; and, after consent, Google for video metadata verification through the YouTube Data API and Google Gemini for transcription. These providers may process outside the EU/EEA. Transfers rely on the safeguards stated in the operator’s approved German policy, including applicable adequacy decisions or EU Standard Contractual Clauses under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR.

7. Storage and deletion

  • Worker transcripts, video metadata, prompts and responses: request memory only; no application content storage or content logs.
  • Installation/request/success records: deleted after successful Reset consent, otherwise after 35 days of inactivity.
  • Content-free installation/network daily counters: current and previous UTC day; not reset by deletion.
  • Anonymous daily success aggregates: at most 90 days.
  • In-flight reservation window: up to 300 seconds. Costs of dispatched work whose final billing is unknown remain conservatively included in anonymous cost totals; expiry is not a refund.
  • Anonymous cost totals and stop state: for operation of the finite provided credit.
  • Local key and settings: until deleted in Alewei/Chrome or extension data is removed.
  • Local summary cache including YouTube metadata: until cleared in Alewei, at most 30 days after the metadata is retrieved.

In Alewei Settings, “Delete cached summaries” clears the result cache. “Delete consent and usage data” requests server deletion, then removes the local installation identifier and consent after confirmation. Alewei displays network errors. These actions apply to data held by Alewei. To delete data stored by YouTube, use YouTube directly. Deletion does not refund provider costs. For access or deletion outside the extension, email the controller and supply the support code shown in Settings. This is a SHA-256 hash, not your secret installation bearer or an API key. Do not send keys, transcripts or confidential content. Content-free admission logs contain only fixed route names, HTTP method and HTTP status; automatic invocation logs and subrequest traces are disabled. Provider platform metadata follows actual provider settings and contracts; request-level store:false is not a zero-retention guarantee.

8. Language and permissions

Automatic, German or English controls the interface and the language of new summaries. Local results are cached by language.

  • youtube.com — identify the video ID from the active YouTube tab’s address and navigate to timestamps
  • api.anthropic.com — create the requested summary directly with Anthropic when you use your own key
  • Alewei Worker — verify public video metadata, run Gemini transcription and the provided summary flow, and enforce quota and spending limits
  • storage — keep keys, settings, consent records and results in the Chrome profile
  • sidePanel — open Alewei and display results in Chrome’s side panel
  • alarms — periodically clear expired local video data
