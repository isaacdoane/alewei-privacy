Decide faster whether a YouTube video is worth your time.

Alewei creates structured English or German summaries in Chrome’s side panel, with clickable timestamps, only after you choose to start.

What you get

  • A one-sentence takeaway
  • An assessment of whether the title’s promise is delivered
  • Key points with timestamps
  • Notes on vague, unsupported or promotional claims
  • A recommendation: watch all, watch a section, or skip

Official release

The free public release of Alewei 1.0.0 is being prepared. A public Chrome Web Store link will appear after technical acceptance and Store approval.

Free extension, limited provided AI credit

No subscription, in-app purchases or advertising. Provided AI access allows up to seven new successful summaries per installation and UTC day, for videos up to 100 minutes, subject to available credit and abuse controls. There is no automatic recharge. You can optionally use your own Anthropic API key; Anthropic may charge you directly.

Missing captions

After separate consent, the video fallback checks the canonical public YouTube URL through Cloudflare and asks Google Gemini to create a timestamped transcript. Hosted summaries then use Anthropic. With your own key, the transcript returns to the extension and is sent directly to Anthropic; your key is never sent to Alewei or Google.

Limits

Automatic captions and AI output may be wrong. Verify important claims in the original video. Alewei does not download video files and is not affiliated with YouTube, Google or Anthropic.
