Alewei 1.0.0 is available in the Chrome Web Store.

Decide faster whether a YouTube video is worth your time.

Open a public YouTube video and click Alewei in the Chrome toolbar. After your consent, Alewei analyzes the video and creates an English or German summary in the side panel.

What you get

  • A one-sentence takeaway
  • An assessment of whether the title’s promise is delivered
  • Key points with timestamps
  • Notes on vague, unsupported or promotional claims
  • A recommendation: watch all, watch a section, or skip

Get Alewei for Chrome

Install Alewei for free from the Chrome Web Store.

Free extension, limited provided AI credit

No subscription, in-app purchases or advertising. Provided AI access allows up to seven new successful summaries per installation and UTC day, for videos up to 100 minutes, subject to available credit and abuse controls. There is no automatic recharge. You can optionally use your own Anthropic API key; Anthropic may charge you directly.

How analysis works

The Worker sends the video ID to the YouTube Data API and receives the title, channel, duration, visibility and live status. It processes verified public videos up to 100 minutes. The Worker sends the public video URL constructed from the ID to Google Gemini, which creates a timestamped transcript. With provided access, the Worker sends Anthropic the information needed to generate the summary. With a personal Anthropic key, the extension sends this information directly.

Your Anthropic key

When you use your own Anthropic API key, the extension sends the timestamped transcript created by Gemini and the information needed for the summary directly to Anthropic. Your key is stored only in your Chrome profile and is sent to Anthropic. Anthropic may bill you according to your plan. Video analysis with Gemini still uses the credit provided by Alewei.

Limits

Alewei processes public videos up to 100 minutes, excluding ongoing and upcoming live broadcasts. Check important claims in the original video. AI-generated transcripts and summaries can contain errors.
